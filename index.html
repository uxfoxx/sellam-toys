Questions

Before you apply

What is the minimum order?

LKR 50,000 per order for retail partners, made up of any sealed cartons. Each product lists its carton size, typically 6 to 120 pieces.

Can I split a carton?

Not at trade pricing, because cartons ship sealed from the factory. Smaller stores can order mixed-case packs of 6 at a slightly higher unit price.

What credit terms do you offer?

Approved accounts move to 30-day credit after three paid orders, with limits based on trading history.

Do you provide compliance documents?

Yes. EN71 or ASTM F963 test reports and the SLSI release for each SKU are downloadable in the portal.
